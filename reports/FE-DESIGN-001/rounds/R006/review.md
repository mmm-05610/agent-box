## Candidate review — FE-DESIGN-001 R006 (digest b6da557a…)

### Overall

I judged the saved candidate text as it stands. The core bet (namespaced resource directory + monotonic replayable stream + same-namespace scoped handles + open CapMap + typed actions) is coherent on identity/isolation (S06), absence-truthfulness (S11), fallback (S07/S12), and no-chat (S04). The R006 reductions (host dedup/reorder withdrawn, spawned set removed via Model2, adapter dedup by its own stable id) faithfully close the three D01 counterexamples and every prior A-lineage row I replayed. However, three scenario trajectories are broken because they claim a catch-up capability the artifact's own two-cursor subscription model does not provide: `from_cursor=cursor.resolve(id)` is defined as "begins live" and `from_cursor=0` as "replays from the first envelope", with **no resume-from-a-stored-position** operation. A view that navigated away or crashed, then resubscribes at `cursor.resolve`, receives only future events — so the missed progress the step says it "replays"/"catches up" cannot arrive. Worse, the deletion-consequence for the ordered-replayable-stream in S03 (the load-bearing justification §R6.6 cites as "S03 catch-up") is attached to a step (S03 step4) that does not perform catch-up, and the step that actually catches up (step1/step5, `from_cursor=0`/read) is not the one tested. This is a real coverage hole, not a phrasing issue.
