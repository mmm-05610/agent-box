# Candidate Reviewer — R002 Independent Verification

## Prose Assessment

**Boundary scan.** The core does not smuggle a service, model, harness, plugin-host or conversation assumption. The `namespaces.open(service_descriptor)` parameter carries adapter identity for namespace minting only; the host performs no service-model operations on it. "Agent", "session", "chat" are adapter-chosen `kind` strings, never host types (§R.1). The `action.invoke` is typed per registration with concrete param/result types — no `execute(any)`, no free-form params (§R.2). The user-pair gesture (§R.4) is a handle-mint affordance, not a standing orchestration harness; the core "records nothing about the pairing." No required burden is relocated into "an extension provides it" without cost: §R.7 itemizes the extension-author requirements for coordinating views and the seven-step adapter onboarding. S08's cooperation burden is explicit (pair gesture + spawned-resource handling) and charged.

**Escape-hatch scan.** `execute(any)` — not present; §R.2 states "Action types are registered per kind with concrete param/result types; no free-form params or results." Omnipotent context — not present; §R.4 lists prohibitions: "It may not: enumerate namespaces, derive a foreign id from a handle, register free-form event names, or hold a global context." Arbitrary event bus — not present; subscription is per-resource via scoped handle, `Envelope` carries resource_id not free-form topic names. Checked against §R.2, §R.3, §R.4.

**Where the artifact is thin.** S02's trajectory is compressed to three steps and does not trace the ongoing "watch tools" loop or the "keep conversing" pattern — the user-observable result implies repeated invoke/subscribe cycles, but the artifact treats them as continuation of step 3's pattern. S07 is a single sentence describing the ordered sequence; it is complete but lacks the numbered-step format of the other scenarios. These are specification density concerns, not broken trajectories.

## Verdict Summary

Eleven of twelve scenarios carry ordered, owned trajectories with stated deletion consequences (either within the scenario paragraph or cross-referenced in §R.6/mech ledger). S09 is self-labeled "Partial" by the artifact: the double-execution prevention is host+adapter joint, and the passage stating the adapter's obligation does not specify host-side detection of adapter non-compliance. This is an evidence gap, not a trajectory inversion.

FE-CE-003 through FE-CE-006 closure holds under replay against the artifact's own stated mechanisms. FE-CE-001 REJECTED and FE-CE-002 CLOSED remain valid. FE-CE-007 cannot get through the saved candidate because the mechanism it targets (edges, edges.list) does not exist in the integrated design.
